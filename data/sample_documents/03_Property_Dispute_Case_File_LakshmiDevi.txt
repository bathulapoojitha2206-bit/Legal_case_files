2. Brief Facts
The plaintiff claims ownership of the suit property by virtue of a registered sale deed dated 9 June 1998 (Document No. 
2214/1998, Sub-Registrar Office, Saroornagar), executed in her favor by the original owner, and has been in continuous 
possession and enjoyment since that date, with revenue records (pattadar passbook and mutation extracts) standing in her 
name.
The defendant claims title over a portion of the same survey number (approximately 150 sq. yards) on the strength of a 
sale deed dated 3 August 2021, allegedly executed by a person claiming to be the legal heir of the original owner. The 
plaintiff disputes the defendant's vendor's title, contending that the entire original holding had already been conveyed to 
her in 1998 and that no residual interest remained capable of being transferred in 2021. The defendant commenced 
construction of a compound wall encroaching on the disputed portion in August 2023, prompting the plaintiff to seek 
urgent injunctive relief.

5. Key Legal Issues
• Whether the plaintiff has valid, subsisting title to the entire extent covered by the 1998 sale deed, including the 
disputed 150 sq. yards
• Whether the defendant's vendor held any residual transferable interest in the suit property as of August 2021
• Whether the plaintiff is entitled to a permanent injunction restraining the defendant from interfering with or 
constructing upon the disputed portion
• Evidentiary weight to be accorded to the Survey Commissioner's report in the face of the defendant's pending 
objections
6. Current Status
The Survey Commissioner's report has been filed and supports the plaintiff's case on the boundary overlap. The 
defendant's objections to the report are pending consideration; trial evidence has not yet commenced pending disposal of 
these objections.

11. Risk Assessment
The plaintiff's position is considerably strengthened by the Survey Commissioner's report, which independently confirms 
the boundary overlap in her favor. The principal risk lies in the defendant's pending objections potentially delaying final 
adjudication, and in the possibility that the defendant may seek to implead his vendor or raise a fresh challenge to the 
chain of title underlying Ex. A1, though no such application has been filed to date.
