2. Brief Facts
The complainant, Sunrise Textiles Pvt. Ltd., placed a purchase order dated 14 February 2024 with Chowdary Trading Co. 
for the supply of 12,000 meters of dyed cotton fabric, valued at 18,40,000, with delivery scheduled within 30 days. An ₹
advance of 9,20,000 (50% of the order value) was paid by RTGS on 16 February 2024 against a proforma invoice issued₹  
by the accused.
No goods were delivered within the stipulated period. On follow-up, the accused furnished a delivery challan dated 10 
March 2024 purportedly signed by the complainant's warehouse manager acknowledging partial receipt of goods — a 
document the complainant disputes having signed or received. Subsequent verification revealed that the transport 
company named on the challan had no record of any such consignment. The complainant alleges that the accused never 
intended to supply the goods and fabricated the delivery challan to delay recovery action and cover the misappropriation 
of the advance amount.

10. Precedents Relied Upon
The following categories of precedent are being relied upon in preparing arguments on dishonest intention and forgery; 
specific citations to be finalized by the handling advocate before the next hearing:
• Authorities on the distinction between a mere breach of contract and cheating, emphasizing that dishonest intention 
must exist at the time of the promise, not merely arise later from non-performance
• Authorities on proof of forgery through comparison of admitted and disputed signatures, and the evidentiary value of 
expert opinion under the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam
• Authorities on the effect of a pending civil recovery suit on the maintainability and conduct of parallel criminal 
proceedings arising from the same transaction
11. Risk Assessment
The complainant's documentary trail (purchase order, payment proof, and the transport company's denial) is strong. The 
principal litigation risk lies in establishing that the accused's intention was dishonest from inception rather than a 
subsequent commercial default, given the defense's likely argument that delivery was merely delayed due to supply-chain 
issues. The forensic handwriting report (Ex. P6) is expected to be pivotal in rebutting this defense.
